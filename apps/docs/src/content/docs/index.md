## Why Flowscript

Flowscript is a visual browser automation extension for people who need more than a simple macro recorder. It lets you compose workflows from nodes, connect them into a directed graph, and run them with native browser actions.

Use Flowscript when your automation needs branching logic, reliable selectors, native typing and clicks, or repeatable execution across modern websites.

## Phase 1: Foundations

The Foundations track is for new users who just installed the extension. You will learn what Flowscript is, how to load it in your browser, how to create a first Google search workflow, and where the important UI pieces live.
